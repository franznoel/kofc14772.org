Membership & Dues

Keep your council membership current and help sustain our shared work of faith, fraternity, and service.

Annual Membership

What your dues support

Membership dues help Council 14772 meet its annual obligations and continue serving members, St. Genevieve Parish, and the wider community.

  • Council operations and member communications
  • Parish, charitable, and fraternal activities
  • Programs that advance the Knights’ principles of charity, unity, fraternity, and patriotism

Dues are assessed annually. Please confirm your current amount and membership standing with the council before paying.

For Current Members

Pay Membership Dues

$35 per year

Pay your annual dues through Stripe’s secure checkout. You will be asked for your name, email, and membership number if known.

Payment information is collected securely by Stripe.

2026–27 Membership Year

Membership Incentives

Help grow the Knights of Columbus and earn special recognition along the way. These limited-time programs reward both councils and individual recruiters for welcoming new members.

Councils & Individual Recruiters

Serve families and bring in new members

Membership & Food for Families

Councils that fulfill their membership quota and participate in the Food for Families program by November 30, 2026, receive eight sets of custom-embroidered Knights of Columbus aprons and hats.

Qualifying councils also earn a chance to win an additional $200 Knights Gear gift certificate.

Go for 2 More

Individual Knights who recruit two men between September 1 and December 31, 2026, are entered into a drawing for $1,500 cash. Ten second-place winners receive customized Knights of Columbus branded items.

Recruit four men during the qualifying period to be entered into the drawing twice.

These offers are for insurance jurisdictions only; other restrictions may apply.

2026–27 Star Recruiter rewards

Recruiters can earn progressively larger rewards based on the number of new members they sign up by the end of each qualifying quarter.

  1. 3 members by the end of the first quarter Earn a “Star Recruiter” Knights of Columbus baseball cap.
  2. 6 members by the end of the second quarter Earn a Star Recruiter hat and Knights of Columbus polo shirt.
  3. 10 members by the end of the second quarter Earn a Star Recruiter hat, Knights of Columbus polo shirt, and jacket.
